Use this service to:

  • get the template for your agreement(s)
  • report your management information to GCA
  • report no business to GCA

If you didn’t do any business, you still need to sign in and let us know.

If you’re having trouble signing in

You’ll need a username and password to sign in to this service. Your username is the email address that gets the notifications.

If you are not able to sign in or do not receive a password reset email, contact report-mi@gca.gov.uk for help.

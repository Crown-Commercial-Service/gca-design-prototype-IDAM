Find legal services for the public sector

Use this service to find legal suppliers. Using this tool ensures you follow public sector procurement procedures.

The tool will identify the most appropriate lot for your requirements, allow you to download and compare supplier pricing, and support your customer journey to downselect suppliers.

If you need legal services for a non-UK jurisdiction, contact info@gca.gov.uk

Before you start

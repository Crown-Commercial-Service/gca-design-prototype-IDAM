What happens next

To use GCA services, your user account must be linked to a buyer or supplier organisation.

What you need to do next depends on whether your organisation is already registered. If your:

  • account is already linked to a registered organisation you can select it and access GCA services
  • organisation is not registered, you can register it or ask a colleague to do so

Note: The person who registers the organisation will become its admin and will manage user access. If this should not be you, ask the appropriate person to register your organisation and invite you.
